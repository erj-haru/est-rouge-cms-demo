To rethink how our corporate website is operated, Est Rouge has started a technical prototype of an AI-native content publishing workflow built on **Astro**, **GitHub** and **Claude Code**.

## Background

With our previous no-code tooling, updates tended to depend on a few specific people. By managing articles as Markdown files in a repository, we aim to bring change reviews, AI-assisted drafting and proofreading into the same workflow as our development work.

![A four-step flow from drafting to review and publishing](../../../assets/news/ai-workflow-diagram.jpg)

## Scope of this phase

1. Type-safe article management with Content Collections
2. Linking Japanese and English articles through a shared ID
3. Generating list and detail pages simply by adding an article file

> In the next phase, we plan to evaluate Claude Code–assisted authoring and automated publishing through CI/CD.
